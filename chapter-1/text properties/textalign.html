Toji Fushiguro is an infamous "Sorcerer Killer" in Jujutsu Kaisen, a former member of the prestigious Zenin clan who renounced them due to the stigma of his lack of cursed energy. His "Heavenly Restriction" gifted him superhuman physical abilities and senses, allowing him to become a mercenary with unmatched prowess, a feat highlighted by his near-defeat of the young Gojo and Geto. As the father of Megumi Fushiguro, he adopted his wife's surname, becoming the Toji Fushiguro who is known for his formidable, cursed energy-free fighting style.
